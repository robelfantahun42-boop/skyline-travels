About Skyline Travels

Skyline Travels is an international recruitment and visa placement agency. We assist job seekers in connecting with legitimate international employers across Canada, the USA, Europe, Asia, and the Gulf region.


Our mission is to simplify overseas employment procedures, provide clear guidance on work permits, and deliver transparent placement services for various industries.
